# Business to requirements
Start with actors, journey steps, pain, desired outcome and accountable product
owner. Separate business outcome from delivered capability; name the causal
hypothesis and outcome validation, not an invented ROI. Distinguish current
state, target, scope/non-goals, fixed constraints, dependencies and assumptions.
Reference supplied evidence by file and locator.

Assign stable R- IDs. Each requirement links an outcome to an observable
acceptance measure, owner, planned evidence and C- controls/components. For
quality scenarios capture stimulus, environment/load, affected resource,
response and measurable bound. Mark each target supplied, proposed or unknown.
Unknown workload/capacity is a discovery action, not permission to invent it.
Identify conflicts and prioritization with product/EA; a score cannot demote a
mandatory constraint.

Choose needs-input only for decisive missing business/evidence; ask 1–3
questions that change the design. Otherwise give a bounded provisional design
with assumptions and acceptance gates. Put journey/trace detail in design.md,
not a new mandatory template.
