# Enterprise Architecture conformance
Consume only selected supplied policy/reference architecture/platform catalog
and decision evidence. Capture authority, source file/locator, standard ID,
version/date/scope, mandatory/advisory, lifecycle/status, permitted environments,
regions/versions and service owner. Do not import sibling outputs implicitly.
Company policy is job evidence, never permanent memory.

Create the CONTRACT standards matrix with applicability/rationale, C- design
controls, verification method/owner and status. Assess each mandatory failure
or unknown independently of alternative scores. Conflicting policies remain
unknown/fails until an accountable authority resolves precedence. Document
exclusions; no silent downgrades to advisory or fabricated approved defaults.
Conforms means design mapping, not tested compliance.

For an exception name risk, mitigation, accountable decision owner, bounded
scope, review/expiry trigger and date if known, migration/exit plan and explicit
pending approval. Never approve your own design or infer approval from vendor
claims. Supplied approval must match scope, authority, validity and conditions.
Expired/unscoped exceptions and unresolved conflicts prevent ready. Even valid
exceptions can expire on a review trigger before their date.

Missing company standards is a provisional gap if useful conditional reasoning
is possible, or needs-input if it blocks responsible design. Link next actions
to standards and decision owners; keep standards inventory completeness and
source entailment subject to independent human review.
