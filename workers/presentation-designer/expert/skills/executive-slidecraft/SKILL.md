# Purpose before layout
For every slide, identify the reader question, supported point and relationship
that explains it. Use CONTRACT.md to retain purpose, chosen medium, design
rationale, notes and claim/message mapping; these are design requirements, not
new schema fields. Plan an intentional sequence of context, finding, explanation,
tradeoff and decision as the evidence needs, not a compulsory dramatic arc.
A defer decision must not become a victorious ending.

Use supported takeaway titles for findings and direct subject/scope titles for
context. Shorten without stripping decision-changing qualifications. One clear
purpose can have supporting detail, but multiple independent takeaways usually
need separation. Stay within the contract's 7-12 logical slides, including any
appendix; consolidate secondary detail into notes rather than exceeding 12.
Put technical depth in notes, not essential caveats. Preserve conditional and
synthetic-study qualifications visibly alongside the claims they govern.

# Match relationship and medium
Choose aligned criteria tables for exact comparisons, bound native charts for
quantitative evidence, small multiples for comparable views, and explanatory
diagrams only for supported relationships. Use imagery only when it clarifies
recognition, spatial meaning or a concept. Map each major illustrative element
to meaning and state its limits. No unsupported architecture, implied measured
topology, causal flow, delivery dates or approved roadmap.

Evidence must remain editable charts/tables/text or supported vector diagrams
in the deck, not a screenshot
of a chart or a visually convincing illustration with retyped values. Bind data
references and preserve chart semantics. Do not invent values, rank, precision
or confidence. If the host cannot provide native editability, report that gap
rather than silently substituting a raster.

Read the updated CONTRACT.md and use kind=effect_plot with comparison_id for
material inferential comparisons rather than dense report-style statistical
prose. Select the actual comparison from source.statistics. The controller draws
the mean and marginal confidence interval, unit axis, zero and designer-specified
practical reference lines as native editable PowerPoint shapes, retaining sample
size and Holm p-value. This is an editable vector diagram, not a native chart
object; preserve source values, SVG masters and provenance through the host.
Keep the required native score_chart and comparison_table as well as the cover
and decision; effect_plot does not replace them.

Put a short audience-facing implication in lead/body, with effect_plot body at
most 35 words. Round displayed results for clarity without changing a material
distinction; keep full precision and longer methods in notes and bound source
values intact. Do not retype or round the underlying data or introduce display
fields outside the contract. Essential conditional and synthetic-study caveats
remain visible rather than relegated to notes.

# Compose a clear first reading
Use one obvious starting point and dominant relationship. Align and group by
meaning before adding boxes; repeat geometry for peers, arrows for real
direction, nesting for actual scope and position for evidenced order.
Apply the shared typography, hierarchy, palette and semantic emphasis.
Use negative space actively. Status needs words/symbols as well as color.

Vary rhythm for narrative function: a decisive statement, evidence, explanation
and comparison need not share the same layout. Avoid bullet dumps, repeating
card grids, ornamental graphics and novelty without explanatory benefit.
Keep copy crisp and leave air around evidence. Split overload rather than
shrink essential type. Choose legible sizes for the presentation setting and
available fonts, then rely on final render review, not inherited canvas units.

# Review the actual outcome through supplied evidence
Check slide-to-slide argument, visible caveats, notes and appendix against the
shared plan and source IDs. Make unknowns, score bounds, preference sensitivity
and confidence read differently. A large number or accent must not manufacture
priority, certainty or a stronger winner.

Use host-rendered previews and fresh controller-supplied critiques when available;
do not author critiques or claim images were seen from text alone. Correct
clipping, tiny labels, weak hierarchy, false visual implications and unsupported
imagery. Keep spec/master, inputs and source mappings for reproducible revision.
Successful deck creation establishes neither native editability nor visual craft
without the host's corresponding checks and complete inspection.

# Decision and visual publication gates
Carry one supported argument through the opening, evidence slides and closing
decision. State alternate conditions as explicit if/then language in visible
copy, not only notes. Qualify status badges so they cannot overpower a stricter
condition. Failure gates must read as failures with supported consequences,
not as ambiguous lists of positive requirements. The closing ask must preserve
the same conditional recommendation and uncertainty as the shared plan.

For practical-importance effect_plot slides, use the selected information
designer's bound reference_lines for the matching comparison_id; do not invent
thresholds or add reference_lines to the slide schema. The zero line answers
statistical difference; the practical threshold line answers practical importance.
Explain the supplied signed threshold, units and favored direction in supported
lead/body text. Prose alone cannot replace the line for a practical-benefit
interval graphic. If a material supplied threshold is absent from the design
handoff, request controller correction rather than claiming wording repaired
the visual. Statistical intervals and missing-score bounds need distinct labels. Separate categorical scenarios
use unconnected points, never polylines. If the fixed renderer cannot produce
the required encoding, flag the capability defect to the controller rather than
rasterizing evidence or claiming the visual is corrected.

In ownership diagrams/infographics, headings must fit their blocks: use short
role names and put qualifications in the detail without losing material gates.
Keep supplied fact IDs in the contract's provenance mapping; raw implementation
IDs are not reader-facing copy.

Read selected prior review and prior spec before revision. Correct all applicable
material content defects rather than rebinding old slides. Require actual fresh
renders and independent quality review of the revised deck, including the
closing decision, legibility and semantic emphasis. Authorship and file checks
alone do not prove publication quality.

# Controller production handoff
When request.json says production=controller, apply all creative and evidence
requirements above to full authorship of output/spec.json. Read all selected
relevant evidence and complete every contracted content element, not just an
outline or old bindings. Bind exact request/input bytes, run the host-supplied
absolute bin/check from the runtime workspace, correct authoring defects and
finish. The host selects an authoring-only check for this phase. Do not invoke
tools/render or claim rendered files exist. The controller subsequently runs
the trusted renderer, full artifact checks and fresh independent image/semantic
review; spec acceptance is not publication readiness. Fresh-render requirements
above are publication gates, not a requirement to render during this phase.

Without that mode, manually operated standalone work retains the default full
bin/check requirement for real artifacts. The authorized host may run the same
exported trusted tools/render after spec authorship. On a rendering permission
failure, report the specific production issue to the controller; do not change
dependencies, redefine renderers, weaken checks or link outside the workspace.

Avoid repeated dumps of duplicated JSON or raw SVG/XML; inspect purposefully
and reserve time to finish the complete authored spec and its check. Conclude
with exactly what was authored, the observed check result and the production
and independent review still owed by the controller, not a readiness claim.

# Respect visible copy and table semantics
Read the current CONTRACT.md, not remembered renderer behavior. Cover body is
visible and totals at most 40 words; use that space for concise context and
material qualifiers. score_chart sidebar body totals at most 28 words. Put each
candidate's name next to its numbers rather than relying on ordering or
"respectively." Explicitly distinguish criterion scores (score 4/5) from counts
(4 of 4 systems), retaining the correct denominator and meaning.

comparison_table supports table_view=criteria for actual criterion scores,
weights and weighted point contributions. The controller includes a signed
first-minus-second contribution for two candidates and paginates at five
criteria and four candidates per page. The default table_view=totals shows
totals, not criterion drivers. Choose the view that supports the argument and
match the title and lead to what that table actually shows; never promise
criterion contributions in a totals-table headline. Retain exact bound source
values and keep material qualifications visible.

Use effect_plot with the actual comparison_id for material interval arguments,
not a dense statistical paragraph. Preserve the interval and practical-threshold
distinctions above and keep the short visible implication and decision-changing
qualifiers within the contracted copy limits. Notes support the presenter; they
cannot rescue a misleading visible claim. Preserve production=controller:
complete and check the authored spec without rendering or claiming that the
produced slides have been evaluated.
