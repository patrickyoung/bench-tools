# Architecture storytelling

Use for north stars, portfolio roadmaps, executive recommendations, investment
narratives, and visual strategy.

## Procedure

1. Start with the audience's decision: who decides what, by when, with which
   evidence and constraints. Lead with the recommendation and consequence of
   no action.
2. Frame the business outcome, affected capabilities/consumers, current
   evidence, uncertainty, and why the decision matters now. Do not turn a
   technology inventory into strategy.
3. Compare a small set of genuine alternatives, including reuse or no-op where
   meaningful. Show trade-offs, risks, opportunity cost, reversibility, and
   owner rather than declaring one inevitable future.
4. Show an evolving 2–3 year direction, not a fixed prediction. Distinguish
   current, transition, and target states; identify ownership, service
   boundaries, retained systems, dependencies, and explicit retirement gates.
5. Pair direction with a near-term reversible increment that can prove or
   falsify key assumptions. State outcome measure, acceptance evidence,
   adopt/hold/stop gate, owner, and review trigger.
6. Make visuals legible without narration. Use native SVG or Mermaid with a
   title, legend, directional flow, named boundaries, state/horizon, and
   ownership. Keep text concise and avoid decorative complexity.
7. For roadmaps, use funding-cycle horizons and show capability investment,
   modernization, debt retirement, dependencies, evidence gates, and decision
   points. Dates are commitments only when supplied and authorized.
8. End with the explicit decision ask, next actions, and unresolved unknowns.

## Evidence

Use supplied strategy, capability/portfolio records, cost and service evidence,
consumer research, decision records, dependency maps, constraints, and dated
owner statements. Label inferred links and unknown horizons.

## Common failures

- A target-state diagram with no current/transition path or owner.
- Fashion vocabulary, exhaustive component detail, or unreadable slides.
- Three-year certainty unsupported by investment and learning gates.
- Roadmap as a delivery backlog or deployment plan.
- Recommendation without decision ask, alternatives, evidence, or next review.
