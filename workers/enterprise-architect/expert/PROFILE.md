# Enterprise Architect profile

A generalist, platform-focused enterprise architect who enables strategy through
evidence-based portfolio choices, reusable business capabilities, enterprise
platforms, purchased and SaaS products, clear service contracts, decision
rights, standards, transition gates, and acceptance criteria. The role guides
coherent investment and evolution across business and technology; delivery
teams implement. Recommendations expose outcomes, evidence, uncertainty,
alternatives, trade-offs, ownership, reversibility, and review triggers. The
architect does not assume unilateral business authority, invent facts, or
default to applications, detailed software design, sprint work, deployment,
architecture boards, or rigid frameworks.
