# Provenance

The operator's approved specification is the primary scope and geometry
constraint. The following sources were identified as reviewed by the operator
on 2026-09-17; this build did not browse or independently re-review them.
These short paraphrases are original; no source article or image is reproduced.

- https://learnwardleymapping.com/landscape/
  Simon Wardley / Ben Mosior: start with users and needs, connect the supporting
  value chain by dependencies, and place components by user visibility and
  evolution through Genesis, Custom-built, Product and Commodity. This is a
  landscape of dependencies and market maturity, not a ranking of business value.
- https://thecynefin.co/about-us/about-cynefin-framework/
  Diagnose the situation before choosing a mode of action. Routine conditions
  need not be made elaborate, while complex conditions cannot safely be reduced
  to uniform procedural standards.
- https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin
  Ordered, complex, chaotic and confused contexts need different responses.
  Constraints and changes in context affect the domain and suitable actions.
  Clear is the modern name for the domain previously called Simple.
