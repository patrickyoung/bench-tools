# Decision-usefulness review rubric

Manual author/reviewer gates, not a model judge, score or automatic semantic
pass. Review the recommendation against supplied evidence and the decision's
scale; revise material failures before submission. Preserve needs-input when
intent is missing. The unchanged bin/check proves envelope/schema integrity,
hash binding and framework arithmetic only, not these judgments.

| Gate | Ready for review when | Concrete failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outcome/evidence traceability | User, situation and problem connect through a labeled benefit hypothesis to an outcome; source locators and unknowns are visible; feature IDs agree across narrative/detail/handoff. | "Build a dashboard to increase revenue" with no problem evidence or mechanism; F-01 changes meaning in the summary. |
| Useful slices | Selected features have a narrow end-to-end useful slice, scope/non-goals and explicit prerequisites; further team refinement is clear. | "Database, API, UI" presented as three user-value features, or asserted PI fit without team evidence. |
| Works versus matters | Acceptance is observable and covers important failure paths; benefit has a measure, population, baseline/target status and review trigger. | "User friendly" acceptance; "shipped" as success; unauthorized access or partial failure ignored; invented baseline. |
| Proportional platform concerns | One evidenced journey anchors relevant contracts, compatibility, safe migration/recovery, support and guardrails; costs/owners can remain explicitly unknown. | A portal rollout without consumer task evidence; forced platform-wide audit for a tiny change; adoption equated with savings. |
| Credible prioritization/discovery | Rationale explains value, risk, dependencies and displaced work; WSJF only uses comparable supplied/agreed inputs with sensitivity; a test can change a decision across value/usability/feasibility/viability. | Made-up WSJF numbers, "more research" with no decision consequence, or ignoring a hard dependency because of rank. |
| Decisive narrative | One recommendation, justified or unknown urgency, alternatives, smallest commitment, proposed owner, explicit ask and measurable review/stop/change rules; readiness is prose. | A feature inventory with no decision, assumed spare capacity or a request to approve the whole roadmap before validating its main risk. |
| Evidence honesty and mode integrity | Facts, hypotheses and proposals differ; limits and conflicts survive synthesis; unknown funding/dates/ROI stay unknown; strategy selection remains not-assessed/null. | Claimed stakeholder agreement, causal savings from adoption, changed vendor winner, or new JSON feature/readiness fields. |

Use only relevant gates: feature detail and platform concerns are not mandatory
templates for vendor evaluation/synthesis. Review those modes for fidelity to
their existing framework, checked selection, statistical caveats and team
boundaries. A short decision can pass with compact prose; volume and framework
vocabulary are not evidence of quality.
