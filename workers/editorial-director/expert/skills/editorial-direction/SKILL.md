# Meaning before treatment
1. Establish the reader, decision, reading context and supplied brand constraints.
   Lead with the supported decision or insight and why it matters; never invent
   urgency. For management readers connect evidence to supplied operational
   consequences, ownership and gates; for executives foreground the strategic
   choice, credible alternatives, exposure and conditions.
2. Trace the central message to checked claim IDs and the PM's status. Build a
   supporting argument, not a forced dramatic story. A defer recommendation,
   unresolved tie or conditional preference must remain such. Separate findings,
   interpretation, hypotheses and proposed commitments.
3. Define the claim/message map in CONTRACT.md's content structure: what each
   important block means, its supplied IDs, support, alternatives and caveats.
   Preserve precise values and terminology. Anchor new plan messages in supplied
   claim IDs under CONTRACT.md; preserve supplied message IDs on revisions.
   Never invent factual source IDs to mask missing support.
4. Translate, do not merely copy, source titles: a clearer title may change words
   but not scope, certainty or meaning. Define preferred terms and non-negotiable
   wording. Keep material qualifications in the executive opening and beside
   the relevant visual, not only in an appendix.
5. Choose a concrete visual grammar: available typefaces and hierarchy, spacing,
   restrained palette with semantic roles, emphasis and sequence. Give unknown,
   ineligible, proposed and observed states distinct words/symbols as well as
   color. Score bounds, weight sensitivity and confidence need separate labels
   and treatments, not one ambiguous error bar or shared confidence color.
6. Consider two or three content-led approaches briefly and choose one for its
   explanatory benefit. Use alignment, proximity, contrast and negative space;
   choose original treatments from supplied references, not artist imitation.
   Illustrations must map elements to concepts and state limits. Decoration,
   large type or polished imagery cannot substitute for evidence.
7. Map the same argument across report, deck, workbook and figures: report carries
   sustained reasoning; deck paces the decision; workbook exposes auditable
   detail; graphics explain comparisons, tradeoffs or conditions. Allocate
   emphasis without erasing decision-changing qualifications in short formats.
   Keep evidence editable and chart values bound to their supplied data.
8. Test the planned first reading against the intended decision and sources.
   Would sequence, size or color imply a stronger winner, causation or approval?
   Resolve wording/design inconsistencies; escalate evidence conflicts to the
   PM/controller. Explain design rationale in the spec, not in reader-facing
   production logs. Renderer capability and visual quality remain unverified
   until the host supplies actual production and fresh review evidence.

This plan is a dependency, not an execution receipt. Express all deliverable
content through CONTRACT.md; do not add another plan file or schema.

# Cross-medium publication gates
Make the opening, visual emphasis and closing decision express the same
conditional argument. Spell out alternatives as if/then statements; phrase
failure gates as failures with supported consequences. Do not let a status
badge, headline or final call to action erase a stricter condition.

Set these medium-specific directions within the existing message plan fields,
not new schema fields:
- Executive summary: 3-4 short labeled paragraphs separated by blank lines;
  default to four: Recommendation, Evidence, Tradeoff, Conditions. Prefer at
  most 150 words; put numerical detail in body sections without losing material
  conditions or uncertainty.
- Workbook: intro at most 450 characters, identifying the conditional
  recommendation and essential caveat. Keep navigation in a short separate
  note within supported content, not a new field.
- Practical-importance figures: captions or subtitles state supplied signed
  practical thresholds, units and favored direction. Never invent thresholds.
  Distinguish statistical intervals from missing-score bounds. Separate
  categorical scenarios use unconnected points, never polylines.
- Word: concise, well-formed paragraphs with readable numerical precision;
  interpret anchors and scenarios rather than reciting every exact anchor or
  all ten scenario results as continuous prose. Checked workbook/data retain
  exact detail. Require a complete sustained report without filler or caveats
  repeated in every paragraph. Refer to figure titles, not guessed pages or
  "below".
- Infographics: ownership headings must fit their blocks; prefer short role
  names with qualifications in the detail. Preserve supplied fact IDs in
  provenance, not raw implementation IDs in reader prose.

Read selected prior review and prior spec before revising the plan. Correct all
applicable material defects rather than rebinding unchanged flawed content.
Pass unresolved production issues to the controller; actual fresh renders and
independent quality review remain release requirements, not claims this plan
or its file check can establish.
