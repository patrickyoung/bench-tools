description: Use when writing a complete executive or management Word-report argument from checked findings, preserving decision status, evidence and the shared message plan.

# Build a sustained argument
Open with the supported decision, recommendation or insight and its consequence.
State timing, requested commitment and decision owner only when supplied.
Preserve decision-changing conditions in the executive summary itself. A proposal
is not an approval, an uncertain estimate is not a guaranteed return, and a
conditional preference is not an unconditional winner.

Outline the reasoning privately before writing: reader question, supported
conclusion, evidence, alternatives, limits and decision implications. Then write
complete connected paragraphs and transitions that explain why the evidence
supports the conclusion. A Word report is neither an outline nor inflated
slide bullets. Give it a clear opening, developed body and supported conclusion;
layer technical depth after a coherent argument.

For executives, foreground strategic consequences, credible alternatives,
financial exposure, principal risks and conditions for success, when evidenced.
For management, explain operational consequences, dependencies, gates, ownership,
cost and measures from supplied records. Do not invent commitments or hide
operational gates in an appendix. Connect technical terms to reader consequences
without exaggeration.

# Edit for meaning and navigation
Use concrete language, descriptive titles, useful sentence-case headings and
an explicit hierarchy suitable for Word heading styles and navigation. Preserve
required exact language; otherwise improve source titles without changing
meaning. Avoid slogans, grand claims, unexplained abbreviations, decorative
punctuation and repetitive section templates. Use precise references rather
than vague "research shows" language.

Every important factual block identifies supplied claim/message IDs via the
contract. Keep numbers, units, denominators, negation, horizons, dates, ownership,
eligibility, weights and proposed versus approved status intact. Compare options
on the same supplied basis. Keep score bounds, preference sensitivity and
statistical confidence distinct in prose as well as captions. An unsupported
conclusion becomes a stated limitation, not a plausible bridge.

Place useful bound figures/tables beside the passage they support, with a
caption explaining the supported read, source mapping, alt text and material
caveats. Do not describe an unseen render as successful or turn illustrative
imagery into evidence. Supporting detail and precise source references belong
in appendices when useful, but decision-changing qualifications stay visible
in the main argument.

# Finish the reader's document
Use CONTRACT.md to express the full editable report content, document hierarchy,
figure placement and references; the host owns Word rendering. Keep production
rationale, process logs, lint findings and tool transcripts out of reader prose.
On revision, target named defects and preserve effective unaffected sections.

Read from summary to conclusion for omissions, contradictions, weak transitions,
unsupported emphasis and terminology drift against the shared plan and other
selected outputs. Consult fresh supplied page critiques for heading isolation,
awkward breaks, figure placement, table legibility and typographic hierarchy.
Textual completeness is not evidence that every rendered page reads well.

# Publication prose gates
Write the executive summary as 3-4 short labeled paragraphs separated by blank
lines in executive_summary; default to four: Recommendation, Evidence, Tradeoff,
Conditions. Prefer at most 150 words while respecting the contract's character
limits. Move numerical detail into body sections, not material conditions or
uncertainty out of the summary.

Carry the same argument from recommendation through the closing decision.
Give alternate conditions explicit if/then language. Do not let an unqualified
status badge or heading overpower a more restrictive condition. State failure
conditions as failures with supported consequences, not ambiguous lists of
positive requirements.

Use concise, well-formed paragraphs and readable numerical precision without
rounding away a material distinction. Interpret exact anchors and scenarios;
do not retype all anchors or all ten scenario results as continuous prose.
The checked workbook/data preserve exact detail. Retain decision-relevant
numbers and units where they support reasoning, while writing the complete
sustained report within the existing section and word-count contract. No filler
or caveat repeated in every paragraph: place each qualification where it governs
the claim, retaining it at the opening and close when decision-changing.

Refer to figures by their supplied titles, not guessed page numbers or "below".
For practical-importance figures, the caption or subtitle must state supplied
signed practical thresholds, units and favored direction; never invent them.
Distinguish statistical intervals from missing-score bounds. Flag supplied
figures connecting separate categorical scenarios with polylines for correction;
they require unconnected points. Preserve supplied fact IDs in contract source
mapping, not raw implementation IDs in reader-facing prose.

Read selected prior review and prior spec before revision. Correct all applicable
material content defects rather than rebinding old prose. Actual fresh renders
and independent quality review remain required, including paragraph flow,
precision, figure references and the closing decision. Neither authorship nor
a file checker alone proves publication quality.
