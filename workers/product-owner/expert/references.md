# Compact primary-reference notes

Selected 2026-09-13. These links are background, not proof that a runtime job
browsed them. Public pages and indexed extracts were used; login challenges
were not bypassed. Dates describe the cited publication/update, and older
canonical guidance is not represented as a newly issued 2026 standard.

- [The Scrum Guide, November 2020](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html):
  public guide/search extract; the Product Owner orders for value and manages
  the Product Goal/backlog, while Developers size and plan delivery. Definition
  of Done is distinct from item acceptance.
- [Scrum.org Evidence-Based Management Guide, May 2024](https://www.scrum.org/resources/evidence-based-management-guide):
  official indexed text; live access was challenged. Separate activity/output
  from customer outcomes and use evidence to test investment decisions.
- [The Kanban Guide, May 2025](https://kanbanguides.org/the-kanban-guide/):
  public guide/search extract; explicit workflow/WIP plus work item age, cycle
  time, throughput and WIP support flow decisions. Forecasts are probabilistic,
  not promises.
- [SAFe Product Owner, January 2025](https://framework.scaledagile.com/product-owner):
  public summary only. Relevant where an organization uses SAFe; it does not
  give a team PO portfolio, RTE, or all Product Management authority.
- [SAFe Iteration Planning, updated 18 March 2026](https://framework.scaledagile.com/iteration-planning/):
  public summary/selected sections only; teams plan against actual capacity and
  historical context. It is optional organizational fluency, not a default.
- [DORA 2025 State of AI-assisted Software Development](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/):
  public executive summary; AI benefits depend on the work system, so adoption
  alone does not establish customer or productivity outcomes.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/nist-ai-rmf-playbook)
  and [Generative AI Profile, NIST AI 600-1, July 2024](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf):
  official indexed descriptions; govern, map, measure and manage risk, with
  human control and failure handling. Guidance is not certification or legal
  compliance.

These are original short notes, not copied publications, diagrams, courses, or
proprietary training. Site refresh and account access remain operator actions.
