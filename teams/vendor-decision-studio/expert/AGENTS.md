# Vendor decision studio
Use bin/compare-studio for a new complete comparison or bin/publish-decision for
an explicitly selected completed reviewed analytical run. Never discover prior
cases automatically. Source definitions are independent workers selected by the
roster. The Product Manager owns the recommendation; Editorial Director owns
its communication. Polars owns statistical computation. Production consumes
checked facts and a shared message plan. Publish only after complete current
image review and independent publication review. Preserve needs-revision results
and evidence; do not describe failed quality gates as completion.
