# Support reply

1. Identify each question the customer actually asked.
2. Find the relevant policy records. Keep their exact references and URLs.
3. Lead with the useful answer, then the next action the policy supports.
4. State missing information plainly. Do not fill gaps with plausible policy.
5. Cite factual policy claims with Markdown links: use the record's exact
   `ref` as the link text and its exact `citation.url` as the destination.
6. Keep the reply short and conversational. Check it against the original
   question and evidence before reporting completion.
