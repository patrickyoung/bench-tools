Guide

Take the long way.
Green on your left, open gold on your right. Follow the lane from the oak, across the stream and up to the high country.

WASD / arrows to walk. Shift for a brisk pace. Drag anywhere in the landscape to look. The lower buttons work by touch, mouse or keyboard. Tab reaches every control; Escape pauses. No pointer lock.

Optional quiet wind, leaves and stream. Sound starts only when you turn it on; canopy passages soften and muffle it.

To remap walking keys, focus a box and press a letter or number. Arrow keys always remain available.

No tasks to finish, nothing to collect. You may leave the lane; the low parapets, bank and tree trunks remain solid. The beaten path beside the bridge reaches the shallow stream.
